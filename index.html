Mahjong Mania

A companion for American mahjong, on your iPhone.

What it does

  • Practice turns learning into a game: short lessons, a daily deal, streaks and a speed round.
  • Helper suggests which hands to aim for and which tiles to pass in the Charleston.
  • Game Night keeps score for your table, with house rules and a settle-up summary.
  • Cards holds your own hand lists and shares them person to person with a QR code.
  • Learn explains the tiles, the rules and the words you hear at the table.

Everything stays on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no network access. Read the Privacy Policy.
