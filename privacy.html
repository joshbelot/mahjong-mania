Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Mahjong Mania collects no personal data.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter or earn in the app (players, scores, game nights, cards, house rules, settings and practice progress) is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. It is never sent anywhere. The developer cannot see it.

What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party code that collects data.
  • It makes no network requests at all.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Sharing a card

A card is shared only when you choose to show or share it. A card's QR code is built on your device from the card's text; someone you show it to can scan it with their own camera, and nothing passes through any server. You choose what a shared file or code contains.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored. You can also open Settings in the app and choose "Reset all data" to clear it and keep the app.

Children

Because the app collects nothing, it collects nothing about children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
