## Description (max 4000)

```
Mahjong Mania is a friendly companion for American mahjong. Learn the game at your own pace, get help choosing a hand while you play, and keep score for your table. Everything works offline and stays on your iPhone.

PRACTICE: LEARN BY PLAYING
Short lessons take you from "what is a Bam?" to choosing hands and passing tiles like you would at the table. Earn stars and XP, build a daily streak, collect badges, try Today's Deal (the same puzzle for everyone each day) and race the clock in the Speed Round. Already know the game? Test out of the basics and jump ahead. Sunny, the tile mascot, cheers you on.

HELPER: HAND AND CHARLESTON HELP
Enter the tiles in your rack and see which hands on your card are closest, which tiles you still need, and which to pass in the Charleston or discard. Track what you have seen and what other players have shown. A Tiles cheat sheet is always one tap away.

GAME NIGHT: SCORING FOR YOUR TABLE
Add your players, record each hand (who won, from whom, jokerless, self-pick, wall game) and watch the scoreboard update. Set your own house rules, from what the discarder pays to a second Charleston or playing with blanks. A settle-up summary shows who owes whom. The app only does the maths: no money is ever handled.

CARDS: YOUR HAND LIST
Use the built-in Practice Card, or type in your own card's hands with a simple notation. Share a card with a friend by showing a QR code. Your cards are yours to keep.

LEARN: PLAIN-ENGLISH EXPLANATIONS
The tiles, the sets, the Charleston, calling, jokers and the words you hear at the table, explained simply, with a searchable glossary. House rules are explained in plain words too.

MADE TO RESPECT YOUR PRIVACY
No accounts, no ads, no tracking and no network access. Your players, scores, cards and progress live only on your device. Delete the app and it is all gone.

DESIGNED FOR EVERYONE
Large text, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion, light and dark modes, and four tile styles, including a large-print one.

Mahjong Mania is an independent app and is not affiliated with the National Mah Jongg League. The Practice Card hands in the app are original.
```
