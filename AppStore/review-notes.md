# Notes for App Review

Paste into App Store Connect, App Review Information, Notes. No sign-in is needed (leave "Sign-in required" off).

```
Thank you for reviewing Mahjong Mania: Companion.

No account or sign-in is needed. The app works fully offline and makes no network requests.

What it is: a companion for the tile game American mahjong. It teaches the game with short lessons, suggests hands from the tiles a player enters, and keeps score for a table of friends.

How to see each feature in about two minutes:
1. Practice tab: tap the first lesson on the path ("Suits and dragons"), tap "Let's go" and answer a question. Tap Today's Deal at the top of the path for a daily puzzle.
2. Helper tab: tap "Deal me a random hand", then tap a tile in the rack to see suggestions.
3. Game tab: tap "New game night", add four sample names, and record a hand to see the scoreboard and Settle up.
4. Cards tab: open the Practice Card (the built-in, original card). Tap "Duplicate" to make your own copy; the copy has "Show QR code", which is how two players share a card in person. Sharing is person to person and offline.
5. Learn tab: plain-English explanations and a glossary.
6. Settings: About has the Privacy Policy and Support links, and "Reset all data".

Money: the Game tab can show points as amounts of money ("Show amounts in money"). The app only does the arithmetic of a settle-up for players who settle up in person. It does not take, hold, send or receive money, has no payments, no purchases and no wagering. It contains no simulated gambling.

Privacy: no data is collected. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or third-party SDKs. Everything is stored on the device.

Content: the built-in Practice Card contains original hands. The app does not include any other card. The About screen says the app is independent and not affiliated with the National Mah Jongg League.

Age rating: 4+. There is no user-generated content shared with others, no chat, no web access and no social features.
```
