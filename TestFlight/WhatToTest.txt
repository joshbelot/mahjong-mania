Thanks for testing Mahjong Mania 1.2! This round turns practice into a game. Please tell me whether it's fun, and where anything felt confusing, too easy or too hard.

What's new
- A new Practice tab. A path of short lessons (2 to 3 minutes each) takes you from "what is a Bam?" through reading the card, jokers, choosing a hand and the Charleston. Earn stars, XP and levels. Mistakes never stop you: they come back at the end of the lesson and again on later days.
- "I'm new", "I know the basics" or "I play regularly" at setup sets where your path starts. You can test out of any unit at any time.
- Daily goal and streak. Pick a goal (1, 2 or 4 lessons a day). Your streak counts your own calendar days, a streak freeze covers a day you miss, and there's no nagging: no notifications at all.
- The Daily Deal: one hand to choose and three tiles to pass, the same for everyone each day, harder as the week goes on. Share your result as text.
- Speed Round (after Unit 6): 60 seconds of keep or pass. Prefer no clock? Settings → Practice → "No timer in Speed Round".
- Free play: the Pick-a-Hand and Charleston drills moved here, with Easy, Normal and Hard, your own card if you like, and "Review mistakes".
- Badges, a profile with a 12-week calendar and accuracy by skill, and a little celebration when you level up. Sounds can be turned off in Settings.

What to try
1. Start with Practice → Lesson 1. Is the next step always obvious? Did anything take longer than 3 minutes?
2. Get a question wrong on purpose. Is the explanation clear? Does it come back at the end?
3. If you already play: test out of a unit from its header and see whether the questions feel fair.
4. Play the Daily Deal and tap Share.
5. Turn on a bigger text size, dark mode, or Reduce Motion and look around Practice.
6. After Unit 6 (or by testing out of it), try the Speed Round.

Known limits: iPhone only, portrait only, no leagues or online play, no reminders. Everything stays on your phone.
