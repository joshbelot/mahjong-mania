Thanks for testing Mahjong Mania 1.1! This round is about things that were confusing the first time. Please tell me where the words still don't make sense.

What's new
- Plain words. The tile picker's toggle now says "Hide tiles" / "Show tiles". "Exposures" are now "Your exposed sets", with an (i) button that explains what that means and a drawn example.
- Charleston. Say if you're East (14 tiles). The Helper shows "Pass 1 of 7 · Right" and holds the 3 tiles it suggests still while you swap tiles, ticking each one "Passed". It moves on only when your hand is full again.
- House rules. Settings → House rules is now a rule book: every rule in plain words, with what it means, a worked example and named choices (never a bare number). Switch rules on or off, add a note for your table, or add a scoring rule of your own. Learn → House rules explained lists them all.
- Blanks. If your table plays with blanks, switch them on in House rules. The tile picker gets a Blank key, and the Helper never suggests passing or discarding one.
- Tile styles. Settings → Tile style: Classic, Traditional, Large print or Modern.
- Share a card with a QR code. On one of your own cards, open the ... menu and choose Show QR code. Someone else scans it with the iPhone Camera to add the card, or use Cards → Import → From a QR code picture.

What to try
1. Hand Helper → Charleston: tick "I'm East", enter 13 (or 14) tiles, and watch the pass tracker. Take out the 3 suggested tiles and put 3 others in.
2. Hand Helper → Playing: tap the (i) next to "Your exposed sets", then add a set.
3. Settings → House rules: open "A jokerless hand pays double", change it to Triple, and read the example. Add a scoring rule, then record a hand in a game night and check the explanation names it.
4. Settings → Tile style: try all four, in light and dark mode and with a larger text size.
5. Cards: make a card of your own, show its QR code and scan it from another iPhone.

Known limits: iPhone only, portrait only, no iPad layout, no 5-player tables.
