Questions, bug reports and ideas are welcome.

Open a new issue on GitHub

You need a free GitHub account. Please do not post anything private in an issue: it is public. Say which iPhone and iOS version you use and what you expected to happen.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data?

Only on your device, in the app's own storage. There is no account and no cloud copy. Deleting the app deletes it.

How do I share a card?

Open Cards, choose a card, and pick Share card to send it as a file, or Show QR code on one of your own cards for a friend to scan. To get a QR code for the built-in Practice Card, duplicate it first to make your own copy.

How do I reset the app?

Open Settings and choose "Reset all data". This clears players, game nights, cards, rules and practice progress.

What are house rules?

Tables play slightly differently. Under Game, House rules lets you set how much the discarder pays, whether there is a second Charleston, whether you play with blanks and more. The app uses them for scoring and in the Helper.

Does the app handle money?

No. "Show amounts in money" only does the maths for you. No money is taken, held or moved by the app; players settle up in person.

Does the app need the internet?

No. It works fully offline and makes no network requests.
